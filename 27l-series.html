27L Series Metal Oxide Varistors

27 mm rectangular MOVs by Guiyang High-Tech Yige Electronic — UL 1449 Recognized, File E335803, with 23 recognized model grades from 82 V to 820 V varistor voltage.

The 27L series

The 27L series is a line of 27 mm rectangular, epoxy-encapsulated zinc oxide varistors made by Guiyang High-Tech Yige Electronic Co., Ltd. for surge protective device (SPD) manufacturers and OEMs. Rated In 10 kA / Imax 20 kA (8/20 µs), the range covers varistor voltages from 82 V to 820 V in 23 model grades. The varistor discs are pressed, sintered, metallized, coated and tested in our own Guiyang plant — not bought in — which is how tolerances and surge ratings stay consistent from batch to batch. In North America, the series is supported by Flyboat Electronic LLC, the factory's sales and engineering center in Bellevue, WA.

How to verify: go to UL Product iQ (productiq.ulprospector.com) and search file number E335803 — the public record lists every covered model. Our MOVs are UL Recognized components for building into end products such as SPDs; they are not "UL Listed" end products. Full coverage for all series is on the Certifications page.

Recognized 27L model grades

Models are designated 27LK followed by a three-digit code. The code gives the varistor voltage: the first two digits, followed by as many zeros as the third digit says — 431 = 430 V, 122 = 1,200 V. (The maximum continuous working voltage of each grade is a separate, lower datasheet value.)

Datasheets and samples

Every grade has a full datasheet (electrical parameters, surge ratings, dimensions), available on request. For samples and design-in support: North America — Thomas Dong, Flyboat Electronic LLC; China — Colin Fei. See the Contact page or the buyer FAQ.

One boundary worth stating plainly: these are bare MOV components with no integrated thermal protection, and they are not drop-in replacements for thermally protected TMOV devices. Disconnection belongs to the SPD design; where a design calls for it at the device level, we supply protection modules with built-in disconnectors — see Products.
