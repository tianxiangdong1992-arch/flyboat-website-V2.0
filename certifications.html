Certifications & UL Verification

UL 1449 Recognized, File E335803 — how to verify our certification and exactly which series and voltages it covers.

UL Recognition — File E335803

Certification wordingUL 1449 Recognized, File E335803
StandardsUL 1449; CSA C22.2 No. 269.5-17 (Type 5 component recognition)
Certificate holderGUIYANG YIGE HI-TEC ELECTRONIC CO LTD
UL Product iQ categoryVZCA2 (United States), VZCA8 (Canada)
Certificate20180514-E335803
North American entityFlyboat Electronic LLC, Bellevue, WA — the certificate holder's North American sales and engineering center

How to verify: go to UL Product iQ (productiq.ulprospector.com) and search file number E335803. The record is public and lists every covered model.

Note: our MOVs are UL Recognized components, intended to be built into end products such as SPDs — they are not "UL Listed" end products, and we do not describe them that way.

This page was last verified against the UL Product iQ record on October 22, 2026.

UL coverage by series

Voltages are varistor voltages. The three-digit model code reads as the first two digits plus the number of zeros in the third digit (431 = 430 V; 122 = 1,200 V).

SeriesUL 1449 coverage (File E335803)
34S31 model grades, varistor voltages 82–1,800 V (codes 820 and 101–182); 430–680 V grades rated Imax up to 50 kA
22S22 model grades, varistor voltages 100–820 V (codes 101–821)
20S22 model grades, varistor voltages 100–820 V (codes 101–821)
27L23 model grades, varistor voltages 82–820 V (codes 820 and 101–821)
30S620 V, 680 V, 820 V, 1,100 V, 1,200 V only (codes 621, 681, 821, 112, 122)
54LOne DC model recognized: 54LK911 (varistor voltage 910 V, DC-rated). No AC models are covered.
25D / 30D / 32D / 40D / 44L / 48LNot UL covered

DC models: File E335803 also recognizes DC-rated variants (model codes with a DC suffix, many also in -U form) across the 20S, 22S, 27L, 30S and 34S series. Three series include DC-only grades beyond the AC coverage above — 22S: 911, 102, 112, 122 (910–1,200 V); 30S: 102, 751, 911 (750–1,000 V); and 54L, whose only recognized model is the DC-rated 54LK911. The full model list is in the UL Product iQ record for File E335803.

Not covered yet? For series without UL recognition today, we can pursue UL certification for your program.
