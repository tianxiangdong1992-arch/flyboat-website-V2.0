22S Series Metal Oxide Varistors

22 mm square MOVs by Guiyang High-Tech Yige Electronic — UL 1449 Recognized, File E335803, with 22 recognized model grades from 100 V to 820 V varistor voltage.

The 22S series is a line of 22 mm square, epoxy-encapsulated zinc oxide varistors made by Guiyang High-Tech Yige Electronic Co., Ltd. for surge protective device (SPD) manufacturers and OEMs. Rated In 10 kA / Imax 20 kA (8/20 µs), the range covers varistor voltages from 100 V to 820 V in 22 model grades. The varistor discs are pressed, sintered, metallized, coated and tested in our own Guiyang plant — not bought in — which is how tolerances and surge ratings stay consistent from batch to batch. In North America, the series is supported by Flyboat Electronic LLC, the factory's sales and engineering center in Bellevue, WA.

Features

  • 22 mm square MOVs, epoxy encapsulated, for SPD manufacturers and OEMs
  • 22 UL-recognized model grades — varistor voltage from 100 V to 820 V
  • UL 1449 Recognized, File E335803 (VZCA2 US / VZCA8 Canada)
  • In 10 kA, Imax 20 kA (8/20 µs)
  • Low-residual-voltage range available for tighter clamping
  • Standard 22 mm square footprint used across the SPD industry

Applications

  • Surge protective devices (SPDs) for AC power systems
  • Power distribution and switchgear protection
  • Industrial equipment and power supplies
  • Surge protection modules and OEM assemblies
  • Communications and data systems

Recognized 22S model grades

Models are designated 22SK followed by a three-digit code. The code gives the varistor voltage: the first two digits, followed by as many zeros as the third digit says — 431 = 430 V, 122 = 1,200 V. (The maximum continuous working voltage of each grade is a separate, lower datasheet value.)

Second-sourcing 22S varistors

The 22 mm square format is a standard footprint across the SPD industry, and the 22S series is widely used as a second source in existing designs. For qualification we provide full datasheets, type-test and routine-test reports for qualified OEM programs, and evaluation samples, with every shipment traceable to its production batch and delivered with a Certificate of Conformance.

Datasheets and samples

Every grade has a full datasheet (electrical parameters, surge ratings, dimensions), available on request. For samples and design-in support: North America — Thomas Dong, Flyboat Electronic LLC; China — Colin Fei. See the Contact page or the buyer FAQ.

Download: 22S datasheet (PDF) — complete ratings tables for the standard and low-residual-voltage ranges, with dimensions and thickness.

One boundary worth stating plainly: these are bare MOV components with no integrated thermal protection, and they are not drop-in replacements for thermally protected TMOV devices. Disconnection belongs to the SPD design; where a design calls for it at the device level, we supply protection modules with built-in disconnectors — see Products.
