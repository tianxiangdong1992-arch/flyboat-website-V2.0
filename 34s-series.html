34S Series Metal Oxide Varistors

34 mm square MOVs by Guiyang High-Tech Yige Electronic — UL 1449 Recognized, File E335803, with 31 recognized model grades from 82 V to 1,800 V varistor voltage.

The 34S series is the flagship line of 34 × 34 mm square, epoxy-encapsulated zinc oxide varistors made by Guiyang High-Tech Yige Electronic Co., Ltd. for surge protective device (SPD) manufacturers and OEMs. The varistor discs are pressed, sintered, metallized, coated and tested in our own Guiyang plant — not bought in — which is how tolerances and surge ratings stay consistent from batch to batch. In North America, the series is supported by Flyboat Electronic LLC, the factory's sales and engineering center in Bellevue, WA.

Features

  • Flagship 34 × 34 mm square MOVs, epoxy encapsulated, for SPD manufacturers and OEMs
  • 31 UL-recognized model grades — varistor voltage from 82 V to 1,800 V
  • UL 1449 Recognized, File E335803 (VZCA2 US / VZCA8 Canada)
  • In 20 kA, Imax up to 60 kA (8/20 µs); high-energy 10/350 µs and multi-chip parallel ranges
  • Centre melt-through design: the puncture point sits under the copper electrode, so a mating thermal disconnector trips faster
  • Eight encapsulated forms — tab, leaded and bare-disc variants for SPD assembly

Applications

  • Surge protective devices (SPDs) for AC power systems
  • Power distribution and switchgear protection
  • Industrial equipment and power supplies
  • DC and photovoltaic systems (DC-recognized grades)
  • Communications and data systems

Second-sourcing 34 mm varistors

The 34S square format is a standard footprint across the SPD industry, and the 34S series is widely used as a second source in existing designs. For qualification we provide full datasheets, type-test and routine-test reports for qualified OEM programs, and evaluation samples, with every shipment traceable to its production batch and delivered with a Certificate of Conformance.

One boundary worth stating plainly: the 34S is a bare MOV component with no integrated thermal protection, and it is not a drop-in replacement for thermally protected TMOV devices. Disconnection belongs to the SPD design; where a design calls for it at the device level, we supply protection modules with built-in disconnectors — see Products.
