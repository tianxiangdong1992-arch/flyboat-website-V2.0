ULTIMA Board-Mounted SPD Modules

Board-mounted surge protective modules in three discharge classes — In 10 / 20 / 40 kA — built on our own MOV chips, with a patented thermal disconnector and remote alarm contact. Supplied for OEM integration.

Board-mounted SPD modules for OEM integration

The ULTIMA series is a family of surge protective modules that mount directly on the equipment’s printed circuit board — no DIN rail, no separate housing. Each module combines our own metal oxide varistor chips with a built-in thermal disconnector and an alarm contact for remote status signaling, in a compact flame-retardant housing that solders or plugs onto the board. The modules are made by Guiyang High-Tech Yige Electronic Co., Ltd. and supplied to equipment manufacturers for integration into their own products; in North America they are supported by Flyboat Electronic LLC, the factory’s sales and engineering center in Bellevue, WA.

Design: the disconnector works from the center of the chip

The ULTIMA disconnector is built around the puncture-point control of our MOV chips. Because each chip’s melt-through point is held in the center of the disc, a sliding thermal disconnector seated at that center point responds quickly and consistently when the varistor degrades — separating the chip from the circuit before it can overheat. The design is protected by invention patent ZL201410061277.5. A status window on the module shows the operating state, and a built-in microswitch provides a floating alarm contact (C / NO / NC) for remote signaling.

Materials

Housing: PA66 (V-0). Inner box and plastic parts: PBT (V-0). Terminals: T2 copper. The MOV chips are pressed, sintered and tested in our own plant; a microswitch provides the alarm contact.

OEM supply — and one boundary

ULTIMA modules are board-level OEM components. They are not UL Listed finished surge protective devices, and end-product certification of the finished equipment is arranged by the customer. We supply the modules with full ratings data, samples for design-in, and batch traceability, with a Certificate of Conformance for every shipment.
