About Flyboat

National high-tech enterprise with over 20 years of expertise in surge protection manufacturing.

Who We Are

Guiyang High-Tech Yige Electronic Co., Ltd. Founded in 2000, with over 20 years of expertise in MOV and SPD manufacturing. Our company successfully completed the intelligent and digital transformation of our production system in 2025. As a recognized National High-Tech Enterprise and a Provincial "Specialized and Innovative" SME, we possess robust R&D capabilities, with our products and services reaching 80 countries and regions worldwide.

Industry Leadership & Standards

For more than 20 years, we have been deeply dedicated to the R&D and production of Surge Protective Devices (SPDs) and Metal Oxide Varistors (MOVs) for board-mounted SPDs. As a member of the drafting committee for China's national varistor standards, we actively participate in the development of industry benchmarks. Our Legal Representative, Mr. Zihao Fei, serves as a Chinese Expert on the IEC SC37B Sub-Committee and Deputy Director of the Varistor Professional Committee of the Chinese Institute of Electronics. He was deeply involved in the revision of the international standard IEC 61643-331 (2017): Components for low-voltage surge protective devices - Part 331: Performance requirements and test methods for metal oxide varistors (MOV).

Manufacturing Excellence

Our facility features a modern plant exceeding 10,000 square meters and is equipped with advanced production lines. We possess robust large-scale manufacturing capabilities, delivering an impressive annual capacity of approximately 30 million standard MOVs, plus downstream production of 20 million onboard SPDs and 10 million finished SPDs built on our own MOVs.
