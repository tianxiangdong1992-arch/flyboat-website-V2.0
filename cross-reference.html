MOV Cross-Reference: Flyboat 34S and 22S vs Littelfuse, YAGEO and TDK

Match a Flyboat MOV to a competitor part by size, voltage and surge class. All Flyboat models below are bare MOVs with no integrated thermal protection.

How to read these tables

The three-digit code is the varistor voltage, not the operating voltage (431 = 430V, 621 = 620V). MCOV is the maximum continuous operating voltage. Imax is the single-pulse peak surge current (8/20 µs). Energy figures carry their waveform: Flyboat figures are 2ms values from the factory manual; YAGEO and Littelfuse figures are 10/1000µs values. Figures measured with different waveforms are not directly comparable. A dash (—) means the value is not stated on this page.

Thermal protection: Flyboat MOVs are bare components. They do not contain an integrated thermal protection element and are not equivalent to thermally protected devices such as Littelfuse TMOV or TDK ThermoFuse. Where a thermally protected device appears below, it is a voltage-class reference only, not a drop-in equivalent.
